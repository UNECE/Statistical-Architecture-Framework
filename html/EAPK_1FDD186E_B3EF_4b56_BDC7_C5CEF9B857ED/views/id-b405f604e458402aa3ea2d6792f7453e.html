SAF GOALS ()
Goals for a statistical office are high-level intentions that describe what the organization aims to achieve, typically in the realm of producing reliable, timely, and relevant statistical data to inform society and policy-makers. In this framework, a "goal" is a motivational element that specifies a desired state of affairs and serves as a guideline for shaping strategies, capabilities, and concrete actions within enterprise architecture

Dat-01 Data is shared property ()
Statistical data that has been determined to add value for use by internal or external users is shared. The CBS Act, article 20, requires us to spend resources efficiently. This implies re-use of data, both in observation to reduce the burden, which is required by the CBS Act, article 36, and in further processing to prevent duplication of work in improving and analyzing data.

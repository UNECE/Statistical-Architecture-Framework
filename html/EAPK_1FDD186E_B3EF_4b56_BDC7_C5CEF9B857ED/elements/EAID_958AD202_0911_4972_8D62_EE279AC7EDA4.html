Bus-01 Maximize the benefits for the organization ()
IV decisions are made to maximize the benefit of the organization as a whole. This principle embodies 'service above self'. Decisions made from an organization-wide perspective have greater long-term value than decisions made from a departmental perspective. To maximize the return on the investment, IV decisions must align with the organization-wide mission and priorities.

Getting Started with SAF

A beginner’s guide. Two processes are followed from start to finish through the steady states, and an exercise helps you map one of your own production processes onto the framework.

TipHow to use this guide

There are three ways in:

  • Read the walk (sections 1–4): one survey dataset and one administrative dataset followed through the six steady states, then the governance cycle that runs around each stream.
  • Jump to the exercise (section 5) if you already know SAF and want to map one of your own processes.
  • Run it as a workshop: sections 1–3 as a 45-minute briefing, section 5 as a 90-minute working session.

The guide assumes you know how statistical production works. It does not assume you know architecture; where a word is used in a special sense, the Glossary explains it. For the ten-minute overview see SAF in Brief; for the full framework see the SAF Framework document.

Like the framework’s business-architecture chapters, this guide stops at the business layer: it does not cover applications or technology.

1. Key concepts

  • Value stream: a sequence of steps that together create a statistical output. Think of it as a production line: VS-01 to VS-06 each add a specific kind of value to the data.
  • Value stream stage vs. business process: the stage (VS-0x.y) says what value is added; the business process describes how your office actually does it. The numbering maps one-to-one, so this guide uses VS-0x.y for both.
  • Steady state: a fixed handover point, where a versioned, quality-assured dataset produced by one stream is consumed by the next. The states are numbered from #0 (source data, still at the provider) to #6 (data in users’ hands).
  • The four process types: every stream is described through the same four groups: Design (decide how it should work), Implementation (build and test it), Execution (run it each production cycle) and Management (check quality and feed improvements back).
  • Business service / object / event: what a stream offers to others / the data it works on / the signals streams send each other (for example “Raw Data Registered” tells the next stream new data is ready).
  • Artefact: what is handed over at a steady state, meaning the dataset plus the metadata describing it. It is immutable: a correction never overwrites a registered artefact but creates a new version. To register an artefact is to catalogue it with an identifier so others can find and cite that exact version. This has nothing to do with a statistical register such as the business or population register.
The steady states of data

ImportantTwo cross-chain disciplines

Metadata everywhere (SAF-P09): value is only added once its context is recorded: what was done, by which rule or method, with what accuracy, and traceable to its inputs.

Confidentiality at several points (SAF-P11): direct identifiers are removed or pseudonymised at standardisation (#2) at the latest, or earlier where that suits the process; disclosure risk is assessed on enrichment (#3); disclosure control is applied to outputs (#4); and a final disclosure check comes before release (#5).

2. The two datasets we follow

The same architecture serves very different data. To show that, the walk follows two threads side by side:

  • A Labour Force Survey (LFS), a case of primary data collection (capability 1.2.1): the NSO designs the sample and questionnaire, runs the fieldwork, and collects both the data and its metadata. The LFS uses a mixed-mode design (CAWI invitation by email, CATI follow-up after two weeks for non-respondents, CAPI for hard-to-reach populations) on a 2-2-2 rotation panel.
  • A tax register feeding business statistics, a case of secondary data collection (capability 1.2.2): quarterly corporate income-tax declarations for all legal entities, received from the tax authority under a data-sharing agreement (delivery within 45 days of quarter end, at least 98% of expected records), for use in business statistics and national accounts.

The tax thread is not purely administrative all the way: at State #3 it is integrated with the structural business survey on the business-register key, and at State #4 register-based and survey-based estimates are composed into one product. This was chosen on purpose. It applies SAF-P08 (data is shared property) and shows how sources that entered the chain separately can be combined once they reach a common state.

WarningThe numbers are illustrative

Volumes, rates, and dates in the walk (42,000 returns, 2,347,891 records, an employment rate of 71.4%, a 09:00 release) are taken from the worked scenarios in the SAF Framework document. They illustrate the mechanism; they are not benchmarks. The tax-thread identifiers (RD-TAX-2026Q3-001 … SP-BUS-2026Q3-001) are the ones used there. For the LFS thread, only OD-LFS-2026Q3 appears in the framework document; the other LFS identifiers are introduced here so the two threads can be read in parallel.

3. The walk, one state at a time

Each state below is one handover card: what arrives, what the stream does, what it deliberately does not do, what it registers, the event it fires, the metadata that travels, and how the two threads differ. The event and metadata rows have the same shape on every card. This is deliberate: a stream only needs to understand that shape, not the work of the stream before it. The cards show each stream’s Execution process; section 4 shows the three other process types around it.

State #1 — Raw Data (VS-01 Data Acquisition and Ingestion)

Source Data → #1 Raw Data. Received into NSO custody in original form, with provenance and basic validation. Nothing about the content is changed here.

State #1 handover card
Labour Force Survey Tax register → business statistics
What arrives Survey responses from the sampled households: CAWI first, CATI follow-up after two weeks, CAPI for hard-to-reach cases. Trigger: survey response submitted. The Q3 quarterly file, delivered via SFTP on day 38 of the 45-day SLA. Before the window opened, the team verified the SLA (≥ 98% of expected records), confirmed the SFTP credentials, and recorded the active agreement reference. Trigger: delivery received.
What this stream does Confirms the round’s needs and sources; activates the legal basis; runs each mode, capturing ingestion metadata at receipt (mode, completion date, instrument version); validates completeness and structure per return; logs every result. The automated pipeline fires on arrival: XSD valid, SHA-256 checksum matches the manifest, virus scan clean, 2,347,891 records (99.2% of expected, above the 98% threshold); 14 corporate IDs not in the business register are flagged but not rejected (known register lag).
What is not done here No coding, editing or change of values: the content is what the respondent gave. No pseudonymisation (deferred to VS-02 by agreement); no conversion of amounts; the XML is kept as received.
What is registered RD-LFS-2026Q3-001: the ~42,000 returns in original form, with per-return provenance and a completeness score. RD-TAX-2026Q3-001: the file in original XML, with provenance, a quality assessment (completeness 99.2%, timeliness on time, conformance pass with 14 flags), and the agreement reference.
Event fired → who listens “Raw Data Registered” → VS-02 “Raw Data Registered” → VS-02
Metadata that travels (both) Provenance, source, collection method, receipt timestamp, file hash, agreement reference, quality assessment (completeness, timeliness, conformance), and a BIV classification (a security label covering availability, integrity and confidentiality; the letters come from the Dutch).

NoteSignature discipline — no data without metadata

State #2 — Standardized Data (VS-02 Data Standardization and Integration)

#1 Raw → #2 Standardized. Aligned to common structure, format, and classifications, with values unchanged. This is the first point at which data from different domains can be combined.

State #2 handover card
Labour Force Survey Tax register → business statistics
What arrives “Raw Data Registered” for RD-LFS-2026Q3-001, ~42,000 returns. “Raw Data Registered” for RD-TAX-2026Q3-001, 2,347,891 records.
What this stream does Converts the file into the target structure; maps education codes one-to-one to ISCED-2011 and region codes to NUTS-3 via correspondence tables; records the survey’s employment-status definition difference from ILO as metadata; pseudonymises the personal identifier. Conformance validation passes, including the no-loss-of-content check (42,000 records, no value changed). Maps the tax authority’s internal activity codes to NACE Rev. 2 (99.4% coverage); converts the XML into the target structure; attaches the statistical business register (SBR) key; records that turnover is in thousands of EUR on a fiscal-year basis as metadata; values are left exactly as received. 14 records without an SBR match are flagged but retained. Conformance passes with every value unchanged.
What is not done here The 42,000 free-text occupation strings are not coded; they are flagged and handed to VS-03 for ISCO-08 coding. The definition difference is not reconciled. Turnover is not converted to units or to a calendar year; the 0.6% of activity codes with no correspondence are flagged for VS-03.
What is registered SD-LFS-2026Q3-001, with mapping lineage to the raw artefact. SD-BUS-2026Q3-001, with lineage to RD-TAX-2026Q3-001 and a note that unit/period conversion remains to be done.
Event fired → who listens “Standardized Data Registered” → VS-03 “Standardized Data Registered” → VS-03
Metadata that travels (both) Source-to-target mapping lineage, applied classification scheme versions, recorded unit/currency/reference-period bases, the list of unmapped or uncoded values for VS-03 to code, definition-difference records, conformance assessment, pseudonymisation record, BIV classification.
What differs Deterministic mapping of coded answers; pseudonymisation of persons. Mapping of an administrative code list to NACE; linkage key attached; the bases of amounts recorded so a later stream can convert them.

Confidentiality step at this state: direct identifiers are replaced by pseudonymous keys before the artefact is registered.

NoteSignature discipline — standardization without loss of content

VS-02 only maps codes one-to-one, reformats the structure, and adds metadata. It never changes a value. Recoding through a fixed correspondence table counts as standardisation because the meaning of the value stays the same. Unit/currency conversion, reference-period alignment, value reconciliation and interpretive free-text coding are left to VS-03.

State #3 — Processed Data (VS-03 Data Editing and Enrichment)

#2 Standardized → #3 Processed. Content validated, edited, imputed, enriched, integrated; derived variables added. This is the only stream that deliberately changes values, and every change must be documented and reversible.

State #3 handover card
Labour Force Survey Tax register → business statistics
What arrives “Standardized Data Registered” for SD-LFS-2026Q3-001, plus the flagged occupation strings and the recorded definition difference. “Standardized Data Registered” for SD-BUS-2026Q3-001, plus the recorded unit and fiscal-year bases and the unmapped activity codes.
What this stream does Error detection flags inconsistencies and outliers; auto-corrections are applied and high-impact records adjudicated. The 42,000 occupations are coded to ISCO-08 (88% auto-coded, the rest computer-assisted and reviewed). Income non-response (9%) is imputed and flagged. Employment status is harmonised to the ILO definition under the reconciliation rule. Outliers and duplicates are detected and corrected with full lineage. Turnover is converted to units on a calendar-year basis (conversion factor and original value stored). The tax data and the structural business survey are integrated on the SBR key, with the reconciliation rule applied where they disagree. Turnover-per-employee is derived.
What is not done here No weighting or estimation: no statistics are computed here. Observed values are never overwritten: the original is kept beside every edit, and imputed values stay distinguishable. Same. Integration does not silently pick one source over the other: reconciliation follows a designed rule and is logged.
What is registered PD-LFS-2026Q3-001: an immutable quality version with complete edit lineage. PD-BUS-2026Q3-001, with lineage to SD-BUS-2026Q3-001.
Event fired → who listens “Processed Data Registered” → VS-04 “Processed Data Registered” → VS-04
Metadata that travels (both) Edit lineage (original value, rule, reason), imputation flags and methods, derived-variable formulae and classification, integration/linkage lineage, quality-improvement assessment, quality-version identifier, BIV classification.
What differs Interpretive coding and imputation of item non-response dominate. Conversion, multi-source integration, and derivation dominate. In both, every change is traceable to its original value.

Confidentiality step at this state: disclosure risk is assessed on enrichment and linkage. In the tax thread, the disclosure-risk report confirms the survey–register linkage stays within assessed bounds.

NoteSignature discipline — full traceability

VS-03 is the domain that changes values, under the guarantee of no undocumented or irreversible change: original values are preserved beside edited ones, imputations are flagged, and every version is reproducible.

State #4a — Statistics (Output Data): analytical outputs (VS-04 Estimation, Analysis, and Statistical Modeling)

#3 Processed → #4 Statistics. The microdata is not edited further; statistics are computed from it, each with a measure of its accuracy, and disclosure control is applied to the outputs.

State #4a handover card
Labour Force Survey Tax register → business statistics
What arrives “Processed Data Registered” for PD-LFS-2026Q3-001, with its imputation flags. “Processed Data Registered” for PD-BUS-2026Q3-001, the integrated business dataset.
What this stream does Confirms the pre-approved approach and current calibration totals. Calibration converges and the weighted margins reproduce the population register. The employment rate is estimated at 71.4% (95% CI ± 0.5 pp nationally, wider at NUTS-2), with variance inflated for the imputed values. Coherence checks against Q2 pass. Small NUTS-2 cells are suppressed. Aggregates are computed by NACE × region. Unstable small domains are estimated with a small-area model and flagged distinctly from direct aggregates. Dominant-unit cells are suppressed. Coherence with national-accounts inputs is checked.

State #4b — Statistics (Output Data): approved product (VS-05 Statistical Product Composition)

Still within State #4. The framework has a single State #4; #4a / #4b is this guide’s shorthand for its two halves, because two different streams work on it. VS-04 produced individual validated estimates, and VS-05 now assembles them into an approved product that readers can use. The numbers themselves do not change.

State #5 — Released Data, then #6 Consumed Data (VS-06 Dissemination and Service Delivery)

#4 Approved product → #5 Released → #6 Consumed. This is where data leaves the office. The key control is a final disclosure check, and nothing is released until it is passed. VS-06 does not alter the statistics; it protects, formats and delivers them.

State #5 handover card
Labour Force Survey Tax register → business statistics
What arrives “Statistical Product Registered” for the Q3-2026 labour-market product. “Statistical Product Registered” for the SBS product.
What this stream does Confirms the plan; applies final disclosure control (two small NUTS-2 cells suppressed); clears the disclosure-risk gate. At 09:00 the figures publish simultaneously on the website, the API, and the dashboard; embargoed media publish at the same moment. Documentation and methodology notes accompany the release; the helpdesk handles queries; usage and feedback are collected. Aggregate tables are disclosure-controlled and published openly. The microdata is de-identified to the agreed standard and made available in a secure research environment (SRE) for accredited researchers, who are onboarded with the data dictionary and methodology. The helpdesk performs output-checking so that results researchers export also meet disclosure rules. SRE usage is logged.

4. The other three process types, worked once

The cards above show the Execution process of each stream, which runs every production cycle. Around it, three other process types run in every stream: Design decides how the work should be done and provides the metadata; Implementation builds and tests the systems and provides the execution process; Management checks quality and performance and feeds improvements back to Design. The cycle looks the same in every stream, so it is worked here once, for the tax thread at State #1.

Design. Statistical designers define the data requirement: quarterly corporate income-tax declarations for all legal entities, for use in national accounts and business statistics. The data model maps the tax authority’s XML schema to the NSO’s raw-data structure: 47 fields retained, 12 derived, the corporate ID as the linkage key. The SLA is set at delivery within 45 calendar days of quarter end, at least 98% of expected records, in XML with XSD validation. Validation rules cover schema validation, referential integrity of the corporate ID against the business register, and amount fields within historical range. The privacy framework records that data is received under the statistics-act mandate, with pseudonymisation deferred to VS-02 by agreement.

Implementation. System configurators set up a dedicated secure file-transfer endpoint, locked down so that only the tax authority’s systems can connect to it. Data engineers configure automated ingestion: file arrival triggers the validation pipeline (XSD check, checksum, virus scan, completeness, referential integrity, range check). Metadata registration is automated: on successful validation a provenance record is created and linked to the agreement, and quality scores are calculated and stored. The pipeline is tested by resubmitting Q1-2026 historical data; one false-positive range check is found and its threshold adjusted.

Execution. This is the State #1 card above: the Q3 file arrives on day 38, the pipeline validates it, RD-TAX-2026Q3-001 is registered, and “Raw Data Registered” notifies VS-02.

Management. The Q3 quality scorecard shows completeness at 99.2% (stable against Q1 = 99.1% and Q2 = 99.3%) and delivery seven days ahead of the SLA deadline. The 14 unmatched corporate IDs are all newly registered businesses, consistent with previous quarters. SLA compliance stands at 100% for 12 consecutive quarters; metadata completeness is 100%. No improvement recommendation is needed this cycle; an annual agreement-renewal review is scheduled for Q4.

Two things are worth noticing. The Design artefacts (the schema mapping, the validation rules, the SLA) existed before the first file arrived, and Execution follows them. And Management ends with a decision about Design, even when that decision is to change nothing.

5. Map your own process

The framework is explicit that “SAF does not prescribe a single operating model, a single technology stack, or a single organisational design”; what it gives you is a shared way of describing the one you have and the one you want. This exercise does that for one production process in three steps. Invite the people who design, build, run and monitor the process; the four process types also make a good attendance list.

Step 1 — Find your steady-state boundaries

Pick one statistical product. For each handover in the chain, answer the questions in the worksheet. Each column is one of the four things the framework says a boundary must specify (the artefact, its metadata, the event, and where responsibility transfers), phrased as a question.

TipReading your worksheet: four symptoms and what they mean
  • You cannot name the artefact at a handover → either the boundary does not exist yet, or two states have been merged or skipped. Both can be legitimate if you can explain why. If you cannot, deciding where the boundary belongs is your first architecture decision.
  • The next team has to read the previous team’s code, or ask them how to use the data → the streams are tightly coupled (SAF-P07). The metadata that should travel with the artefact is living in people’s heads or in scripts.
  • The same data is re-collected or re-cleaned by a second team → the artefact is not shared property (SAF-P08). This is usually a problem of registration or discovery rather than a technical one.
  • Values change between #1 and #2 → the standardisation boundary is in the wrong place. Anything that changes a value belongs in VS-03, where every change is logged against its original.

A boundary is an agreement between streams and does not have to match an organisational unit. One team may own several states, and a central unit may run processing for many domains. The agreement in each row just needs to be written down.

TipScoring in a 90-minute workshop

Ten minutes per stream column, with the people who design, build, run, and monitor that stream in the room. Score by consensus; when the room disagrees, record the lower score and the reason. Precision matters less than the overall pattern. A common result is a stream scoring 2 on Execution but 0 on Design and Management: it runs well, but without design or management artefacts it cannot be changed safely.

Any cell scoring 0 or 1 points you at the stream’s full checklist in the SAF Framework document (the Maturity Assessment Checklist under Applying This to Your NSO in that stream’s deep dive), where twenty detailed questions describe what “systematic” looks like.

SAF Architecture Framework

This is the full Statistical Architecture Framework document, split into chapters. It is the reference: everything the workgroup has agreed about how a statistical office can describe its production architecture. You do not need to read it in order, and most readers never read all of it.

If you are new to SAF, start with SAF in Brief instead, then come back here for the chapter you need. Terms are explained in the Glossary.

The chapters

The foundations — why SAF exists and what it is built on.

Chapter What it covers Who needs it
Introduction and Scope What SAF is for, what it covers and what it deliberately does not prescribe, and who it is written for Everyone, first
Motivation The pressures driving change, the goals, the eleven architecture principles, the outcomes and the stakeholders Managers, policy makers, privacy officers
Strategy The capability model (what an office must be able to do) and the ten value streams Architects, planners

The business architecture — how the production chain actually works. This is the part most readers come for.

Chapter What it covers Who needs it
Business Architecture The steady states of data, the four-process governance pattern, how the principles apply across the chain, and a summary of each value stream Everyone working with production

The ten value streams in depth — one chapter each. Each follows the same shape: purpose, position in the chain, the steady state it produces, the four process types, business services and boundary contracts, events, framework mappings, a maturity checklist and worked scenarios.

The six production streams, in the order data passes through them:

Chapter Takes data from → to
VS-01 Data Acquisition and Ingestion Source data → #1 Raw
VS-02 Data Standardization and Integration #1 Raw → #2 Standardized
VS-03 Data Editing and Enrichment #2 Standardized → #3 Processed
VS-04 Estimation, Analysis, and Statistical Modeling #3 Processed → #4 Statistics
VS-05 Statistical Product Composition Within #4, to an approved product
VS-06 Dissemination and Service Delivery #4 → #5 Released → #6 Consumed

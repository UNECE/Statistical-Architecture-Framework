# Skill set: Enterprise Architect (decades of experience)

## Profile

Enterprise architect with more than 30 years of experience, from software development and infrastructure to board-level architecture. Combines deep technical knowledge with a broad view across business, information, applications, and technology. Turns strategy into executable architecture and can defend a design both in the boardroom and at the whiteboard with engineers.

## 1. Strategy and governance

- Translating business strategy into target architecture, roadmaps, and investment proposals
- Setting up and leading architecture functions, architecture boards, and design authorities
- Portfolio management and application rationalization
- Defining architecture principles, standards, and reference architectures
- Business cases, TCO analysis, and DevOps
- Due diligence and IT integration for mergers, acquisitions, and divestitures
- Vendor and sourcing strategy, contract review from a technical perspective
- Driven by Open Source and digital sovereignty 
- Managing technical debt and legacy risk

## 2. Architecture domains

**Business architecture**
- Capability mapping, value streams, and operating models
- Process architecture and organization design in relation to IT

**Information and data architecture**
- Data strategy, data governance, and master data management
- Conceptual, logical, and physical data modeling
- Data warehouse, data lake, data lakehouse, and data mesh
- Data quality, lineage, and metadata management

**Application architecture**
- Monoliths, SOA, microservices, and modular monoliths; knowing when each fits
- Domain-Driven Design, event-driven architecture, CQRS, and event sourcing
- Package selection and implementation of core systems
- Legacy modernization, large data migrations

**Technology architecture**
- Data center, network, and platform architecture
- Hybrid and multi-cloud designs
- Availability, scalability, disaster recovery, and business continuity

## 3. Deep technical expertise

**Cloud and platform**
- Private-, Hyperscaler Cloud: landing zones, networking, identity, and cost control
- Containers and Kubernetes, serverless, platform engineering
- Infrastructure as Code and GitOps

**Integration**
- API strategy and API management (REST, GraphQL, gRPC)
- Messaging and streaming (Kafka, RabbitMQ, cloud-native brokers)
- Integration patterns, ESB, iPaaS, B2B/EDI, and file-based interfaces

**Data and analytics**
- Relational and NoSQL databases, query optimization, and transaction models
- Batch and streaming pipelines, orchestration, open table formats
- BI, analytics platforms, and MLOps

**Security**
- Zero trust, identity and access management, federation (OAuth 2.0, OIDC, SAML)
- Threat modeling, security by design, and privacy by design
- Encryption, key management, privacy preserving technology, network segmentation
- DevSecOps and supply-chain security

**Software engineering**
- Hands-on background in multiple languages and paradigms (including Java, C#, Python, SQL)
- Design patterns, clean architecture, test strategy
- CI/CD, release strategies, observability, and SRE principles
- Performance engineering and capacity planning

**AI and emerging technology**
- Architecture for machine learning and generative AI (RAG, MCP, agents, model governance)
- Assessing new technology for maturity, risk, and value
- Responsible AI and EU AI Act compliance

## 4. Frameworks, methods, and notations

- TOGAF, Zachman, ArchiMate and derived reference architectures
- C4 model, UML, BPMN
- Architecture Decision Records, ATAM, and fitness functions
- Agile architecture: SAFe, LeSS, Scrum, DevOps, Team Topologies
- ITIL, COBIT, IT4IT
- ISO 27001, NIST CSF, CIS Controls

## 5. Laws and regulations

- GDPR, NIS2, DORA, EU AI Act, Data Act
- Sector-specific requirements (finance, government, healthcare, industry)
- Translating compliance requirements into architecture controls and audit evidence

## 6. Leadership and collaboration

- Trusted advisor at C-level; explains complex subjects clearly and briefly
- Stakeholder management and decision-making under conflicting interests
- Guiding large transformation programs
- Coaching and developing architects and lead engineers
- Facilitating workshops (event storming, capability mapping, risk sessions)
- Writing: architecture documents, decision papers, board memos
- Negotiating with vendors and partners

## 7. Defining qualities

- Pragmatism: picks the simplest solution that meets the requirements
- Systems thinking: sees consequences across domains, teams, and years
- Judgment built on experience: recognizes patterns, hype, and pitfalls
- Willing to say no and backs it up with facts
- Stays technically current and still builds prototypes
